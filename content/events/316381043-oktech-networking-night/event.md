**OKTech Year two networking!** 😎

The first year of OKTech has passed, lot's of things have happened, let's celebrate! 🥂

Meet us for _non-alcoholic drinks_ and some _light networking_ at a special venue! Besides **meeting interesting people working in the Osaka tech industry, we will also share a little bit of history with you about what happened in the past years, how the logo and homepage came together and we will add some technical notes as well.

Is this a paid event? Yes, 1500yen, but **two drinks are included with the price**! 🍸 Also, there will be some snacks!

While most of the evening will be talking to our friends we plan to have a few intermissions (_Agenda: TBD_).

Feel free to bring your **international and Japanese friends** with you, anyone with an interested in tech is welcome. We would be happy if you could join and celebrate with us our the first official **OKTech** Meetup!

Until then, see you in [Discord](https://oktech.jp/discord)!
